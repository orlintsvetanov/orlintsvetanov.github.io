BIOGRAPHY · 2012–2025

Orlin Tsvetanov and the Sofia Soloists

I was a violinist in the Sofia Soloists chamber ensemble from 2012 to 2025. My work with the ensemble is part of my journey as a musician.

More about meContact

MY JOURNEY

Chamber music and tradition

My path to the ensemble began after a Lili Ivanova concert at Arena Armeec in 2012, when colleagues from the Sofia Soloists invited me to join. I spoke about this in an interview with Bulgarian National Radio in 2024.

In an interview marking the ensemble’s 60th anniversary in 2022, I spoke about the value of a tradition passed between generations and of performing works by Bulgarian composers.

Among my announced appearances as a soloist with the ensemble was the opening of the Chamber Music Days festival in Gabrovo in 2020, listed in the municipality’s programme.
