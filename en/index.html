My performing career spans stages and genres: classical, jazz, flamenco, tango and pop. I bring that experience and my own interpretation to every encounter with an audience.

Read my biography

Music has been part of my life since childhood: I grew up in a family of musicians. I studied at the Lyubomir Pipkov National School of Music and the Prof. Pancho Vladigerov National Academy of Music, in Prof. Angel Stankov's violin class. In 2003, I specialised in Baroque music in Germany with Prof. Anne Schumann.

I have performed as a violinist with Lili Ivanova since 2005. From 2012 to 2025, I was a member of the Sofia Soloists Chamber Ensemble. As a soloist and chamber musician, I have performed in Japan, China, France, Germany, Austria, Spain and other countries.

My projects include Sonata Duo with pianist Marin Gonev, Classical Meets Jazz with my sister Zornitsa Tsvetanova, and Tango Cats with Mladen Taskov, established in 2017. Zornitsa and I released our album Dream of OZ in 2016. I am also one of the founders of Bingo Symphony Orchestra.

I was a prizewinner at the International French Music Competition in Plovdiv in 1999. I have appeared at Varna Summer, Apollonia, the Bansko International Jazz Festival and Jazz Fest Pamporovo. In 2018, I gave masterclasses in China.

My professional experience includes work with the New Symphony Orchestra, Sinfonietta Sofia and the Orpheus Chamber Orchestra in Pernik. I have performed with musicians including Zhivko Petrov, Stoyan Yankoulov, Mihail Yosifov, Veselin Veselinov-Eko and Zhivko Vasilev.

Technical preparation and organisation

What I provide

I provide stage attire, an acoustic or electric violin and a prepared repertoire. I use a computer with pre-mixed instrumental backing tracks and a small mixer. I send a stereo signal, left and right channels, to the sound system through two cables.

What the organiser provides

A suitable sound system and a sound engineer. Travel costs and any necessary accommodation are covered separately from the performance fee.

Terms and frequently asked questions

How do I pay for lessons?

After you book a time, we’ll agree by email whether you would like a single lesson or a package and how to pay. No online payment is required in the calendar.

Do packages have an expiry date?

Packages do not expire. If you take a break, your unused lessons remain available.

When and how often do lessons take place?

Lessons are available Monday to Friday between 17:00 and 19:00, Bulgarian time, subject to availability. One lesson a week is a good starting point; we agree on the pace individually.

How long is a lesson?

Every regular lesson, whether booked individually or in a package, lasts 45 minutes. The trial session lasts 15-20 minutes and introduces you to the way we work.

Where do online lessons take place?

Lessons take place on Discord or another app that works for both of us. We’ll arrange the call connection by email after booking.

What do I need for an online lesson?

A violin and bow, a computer with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection and a quiet place to play. Position your camera so that your instrument and both hands are visible. Specialist studio equipment is not required.

Are the lessons suitable for beginners?

Lessons are adapted to your level, including if you have no previous musical training. If you do not yet own an instrument, please discuss your choice with me before the first lesson.
