The free trial meeting lasts 15–20 minutes. We discuss your goals and how we work. Please call to arrange it.

Regular times are Monday to Friday, 17:00–19:00 Bulgarian time, subject to availability. Please call to discuss the price of a lesson or package. We agree on packages and payment after booking.

Questions about lessons

Are the lessons suitable for beginners?

Yes. Lessons are adapted to your level, including if you are starting without musical training.

What do I need for an online lesson?

A violin and bow, a device with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection and a quiet place to play.

How do I book a regular lesson?

Choose an available time in the calendar. After booking, we will agree on the package, payment and lesson connection by email.
