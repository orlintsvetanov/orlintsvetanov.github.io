THE FORMAT

Solo or with guest musicians

I play acoustic or electric violin with contemporary instrumental backing. Depending on the occasion, performances with piano, kaval, harp or a larger ensemble are also possible.

We can plan an hour, an hour and a half or two hours of music. We agree on the set structure, repertoire and terms for the event.

Practical questions

What sound equipment is needed?

The organiser provides a suitable sound system and sound engineer. I provide a stereo signal from a small mixer.

Can you perform outside Sofia?

Travel is discussed for each event. Travel costs and any necessary accommodation are separate from the fee.

How is the fee determined?

Please call to discuss the performance fee and terms.
